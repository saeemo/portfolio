# Risk Assessment Methodology

## Method
1. Identify business and technology assets.
2. Identify plausible threats and vulnerabilities.
3. Record existing controls.
4. Rate **Likelihood** from 1 to 5.
5. Rate **Impact** from 1 to 5.
6. Calculate **Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**.
7. Select a treatment: Mitigate, Avoid, Transfer, or Accept.
8. Assign an owner and action plan.
9. Re-score residual likelihood and impact.

## Risk Bands
- Low: 1–4
- Medium: 5–9
- High: 10–15
- Critical: 16–25
