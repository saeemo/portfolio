# Information Security Policy — FinCore

## Purpose
Establish management direction for protecting information within the simulated ISMS.

## Policy Principles
- Information must be classified and protected according to business sensitivity.
- Access must follow least privilege and business need.
- Security risks must be identified, assessed, treated, and reviewed.
- Security incidents must be reported and handled through an approved process.
- Legal, regulatory, contractual, and internal security requirements must be considered.
- Security responsibilities must be assigned and reviewed.

## Review
This policy is reviewed at least annually or after a significant change.
